Method and coverage note (read first): Collected 2026-11-15 using Firecrawl web search with `site:` operators (about 20 search calls, roughly 700 raw results, de-duplicated). Every entry below comes from a search-index snippet. I did not open each video, so:
- "Creator" is filled only when the handle or name shows in the URL or snippet. When a YouTube snippet names a creator only as a "related/up-next" video, the entry says "(referenced in sidebar)" and has no direct URL.

X posts are indexed patchily, and LinkedIn mainly through public `/posts/` pages.
- Follower and subscriber counts are as shown in the snippet or on third-party stats pages on the date of collection. Treat them as approximate.
- Many snippets mention model names such as "Claude Fable 5", "Opus 4.8/5.5", "GPT-5.6". These are copied verbatim from the sources and were not checked.

## Q1. Which reels and videos demo building a company brain or second brain (n8n, Claude, NotebookLM, Obsidian, GBrain, Notion AI, etc.)?

### Takeaway
"Second brain / company brain" content in 2025-2026 is dominated by one recipe: Claude Code (or Claude Cowork) plus an Obsidian/markdown vault, often framed as "Karpathy's LLM Wiki". n8n + Supabase/vector-store RAG agents come second, then Notion AI agents, NotebookLM, and Garry Tan's open-source GBrain (released April 2026). Instagram reels mostly use a "Comment BRAIN and I'll DM you the guide" funnel. YouTube carries the full walkthroughs, and LinkedIn carries the vendor/enterprise "company brain" framing (Glean-style context layers, knowledge graphs, Cognee, Lyzr, Atlan, Sentra).
