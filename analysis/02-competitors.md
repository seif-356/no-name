## Takeaways

1. Nobody visibly makes money from this as pure software; public players lose money. Don't play the outspend game.
2. Setup fees are normal (Sana $45–65k; consultancies $25–75k for 50 people). Charge for cleanup separately.
3. Funded Egyptian players sell to banks, telecoms and government. Small Egyptian companies are unserved, and also the lowest-paying segment.
4. Nobody in the Middle East markets an Arabic-first internal company brain.
